# Eunice

> Eunice is a London-based AI fintech building institutional-grade due diligence and monitoring infrastructure for digital assets and private markets — automating token due diligence, classification, risk analysis, and disclosure (including an AI-powered MiCA whitepaper library), delivered via dashboard and API to exchanges, funds, custodians, issuers, and regulators.
